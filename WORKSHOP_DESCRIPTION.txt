[h1]Unlimited Mercenaries (Player Only)[/h1]

Removes the artificial scarcity from [b]every[/b] mercenary-pool mechanic in the game — for the human player only. AI factions are untouched.

[h2]What it does (player factions only)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every mercenary-pool unit gets [b]+30 pool capacity[/b]
[*]Stock refills to cap on every save load, plus +1/turn native replenishment and +2/turn scripted top-up
[*]Scripted unlock conditions (fealty, tech, buildings, missions) are lifted — hire from turn one
[*]Per-army mercenary limit raised by [b]+17[/b] (3 → 20, 5 → 22)
[*]Faction-wide caps of capped special units (Eltharion's Mistwalkers etc.) raised by +17
[*]Hire costs are untouched: gold and special-resource prices stay vanilla
[/list]

[h2]Covered mechanics — 327 units across 22 pools[/h2]
[list]
[*]Dwarfs: Book of Grudges reconcilers, Malakai's adventure units
[*]Empire: Elector Counts units, Imperial Supply (Wulfhart), Elspeth's Amethyst units + state troops unlocks
[*]Greenskins: Waaagh! bands
[*]Lizardmen: Blessed Spawnings (incl. Cult of Sotek rites)
[*]Dark Elves: Rakarth's Monster Pen
[*]Skaven: Throt's Flesh Lab, Ikit's Workshop prototypes, Snikch's doppelganger triads
[*]High Elves: Eltharion's Mistwalkers (recruitment caps)
[*]Chaos: daemonic summoning pools (WoC, Be'lakor), Nurgle unit pools, Slaanesh daemonic attraction
[*]Ogre Kingdoms: ogre camps
[*]Norsca: Monstrous Arcanum
[*]Cathay: Shang-Yang support
[*]Kislev: Zealous Conscription
[/list]

[h2]Not touched[/h2]
Regiments of Renown (unique named regiments), raise-dead pools (province mechanics), Tamurkhan's chieftains (story companions).

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Save-game compatible: add or remove at any time
[*]Unit cards can show "vanilla cap + 30" (31/32...) — the mod's pool entry stacks with the vanilla one, cosmetic only
[*]The per-army limit label shows the base value; the actual enforced limit includes the +17 bonus
[*]Units granted at the very moment of campaign creation (e.g. Elspeth's state troops) keep their vanilla cap, but their stock is topped up every turn
[/list]
